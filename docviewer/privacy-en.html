Information processed

The app has no account registration, ads or proprietary analytics server. Document files and recognized text are processed on your device. Google ML Kit and Google Play services, used to search scanned PDFs, may process technical information for diagnostics and usage statistics.

This information may include device manufacturer, model and operating system; app package and version; device or installation identifiers; recognition processing time; input image format and resolution and input/output sizes; SDK version; model download and recognition events; and error codes. According to Google, transmission is encrypted using HTTPS, and document images and recognized text themselves are not sent to Google servers.

For details, see the Google ML Kit Android data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy linked below.

Document files

Documents you open are read and displayed on your device. The app does not directly upload them to a server. If you choose “Send to Gemini” or “Share document”, the app grants the selected app read access to the original document. The receiving app may upload it to a server under that service’s own policies.

Information stored on your device

Scanned PDF search and highlights use Google ML Kit to recognize Korean, English, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, French and German text on-device. Only the needed models, from Latin, Korean, Chinese, Japanese and Devanagari, are downloaded through Google Play services on first use. Each model’s first download may require an internet connection; subsequent recognition stays on-device. Documents and recognized text are not sent to a server. Images used for search are released from memory after processing.

The recent-file list (file names and locations) is stored in private app storage on your device so you can reopen documents. Up to 30 entries are retained; opening new files removes the oldest entries. Clearing app data in device settings or uninstalling the app deletes the list. The app does not delete your original documents; you can delete them in a file manager. Automatic backup of app data is disabled.

Third-party sharing

Only when you use a sharing feature is the original document passed to the selected app. Otherwise, document files and recognized text are not provided to third parties. Technical information processed by Google services for OCR and model delivery is covered by the information section above and Google’s Privacy Policy.
